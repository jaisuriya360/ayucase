1. System Architecture & Core Concepts

AyuCase is an AI-Synthesized Patient Narrative Architecture. It is designed to bridge the communication gap between patients and clinicians by transforming unstructured natural patient speech, documents, and medical milestones into clean, structured clinical intelligence.

Key Architectural Pillars
  • Dual-Sided Unified Portals: Seamless split between the Patient Experience and the Physician Clinical Workstation.
  • Zero-Knowledge Demo Sandbox: All patient data operates purely in browser local memory (LocalStorage) with synthetic demonstration personas (Aarav Chen & Dr. Vance). No external biometric or national ID servers are accessed.
  • Interactive 3D Biological Visualization: Utilizes Three.js and @react-three/fiber to render high-fidelity anatomical models and symptom hot-spots.
  • Multilingual Speech & Narrative: First-class support for 6 Indian Languages (English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali).

2. Answers to Your Doubts (Essential User Questions)

Doubt 1: Why does the Chrome shortcut close when the IDE closes? Core Knowledge

Why it happens: The link http://localhost:5173 points to a server running inside your computer's RAM. In developer mode, that server was started by your Antigravity IDE session. When you close the IDE, Windows terminates that process. Thus, Chrome cannot connect to a server that has been shut down.

Two Permanent Solutions:

  • Solution A (One-Click Offline Desktop Launcher): We have created a dedicated launcher file in your project folder named start-ayucase.bat. Whenever you want to use the website without opening Antigravity IDE, simply double-click start-ayucase.bat. It automatically boots the web server in 2 seconds and opens your Chrome browser to http://localhost:5173/!
  • Solution B (Permanent 24/7 Cloud Website): You can deploy the website to free high-speed hosting (like Vercel or Netlify) with 1 click. This gives you a live permanent web address (e.g., https://ayucase.vercel.app) that works on any phone, tablet, or laptop 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, even when your PC is completely turned off! (See Section 10).
Doubt 2: How do I proceed with making small/minor changes to the site? Pair Programming

You don't have to write any complex code yourself! You can simply tell me in the chat whatever you want changed.

Recommended Procedure to Request Changes:

1
Specify the Page Name

Tell me which page you are looking at (e.g., Landing Page, Patient Interview, Physician Radar, or Elder Mode).

2
List Your Desired Changes

Give bullet points of what to adjust: button text, colors, layouts, adding fields, removing sections, or changing sample patient names.

3
Instant Hot-Reload Validation

Because the Vite dev server uses Hot Module Replacement (HMR), the changes appear in your browser within milliseconds without losing your page state!

5. AI Pre-Interview Voice Pipeline (Key Feature)

The pre-interview module replaces intimidating medical intake paperwork with an intelligent conversational experience.

Real-Time Voice-to-Structure Processing Patent-Grade Architecture

The interface guides the user through 5 standard clinical intake questions:

  1. Chief Complaint: Primary symptom or reason for visit (e.g. throbbing unilateral headache).
  2. Onset & Duration: When the symptoms started and how long episodes persist.
  3. Aggravating / Relieving Factors: Trigger factors such as bright light, stress, or caffeine.
  4. Current Medications & Supplements: Active prescription or OTC intake.
  5. Associated Symptoms: Nausea, visual disturbances, or dizziness.
Interactive Elements on this Screen:
  • Mic Button: Simulates live audio capture with dynamic audio waveform bars.
  • Type with Keyboard: Toggle switch to allow manual text typing instead of voice.
  • Audio Read-Aloud: Uses browser speech synthesis to read the question aloud in the patient's language.
  • Entity Extraction Pill: As the patient answers, the system automatically parses and displays: Clinical Field, Standardized Value, Confidence Score (e.g. 97%), and Clinical Classification Tags.
  • Generate Pre-Visit Summary: Opens a modal presenting a formatted physician handoff summary.

8. Contradiction Radar Engine (Safety & Anomaly Detection)

One of AyuCase's signature clinical innovations is the Contradiction Radar (/physician/contradictions). It continuously scans patient intake answers against their existing medical records to detect dangerous discrepancies.

Sample Detected Discrepancies Clinical Safety
  1. Medication Conflict (Ergotamine vs Triptans): Alerts the doctor if a patient reports taking an over-the-counter migraine remedy that interacts with a prescribed abortive medication.
  2. Vitals vs Symptom Disconnect: Flags situations where patient reports feeling severe palpitations, but recorded ECG telemetry and resting heart rate indicate 68 bpm (sinus rhythm).
  3. Allergy & Penicillin Anomaly: Flags if past discharge documents list "No Known Allergies" while recent intake answers mention hives after Amoxicillin.
Physician Action Stances:
Doctors can click "Accept / Clarify with Patient", "Mark as False Positive", or "Override & Document". Every action is recorded in the permanent audit trail.

10. How to Make AyuCase a Full-Time 24/7 Live Website

Currently, AyuCase runs on your computer at http://localhost:5173/. To make it a permanent website accessible anywhere in the world 24/7 (even when your PC is turned off), follow these simple steps:

Option 1: Deploy to Vercel (100% Free, Recommended) 1 Minute Setup
1
Open Terminal in AyuCase Directory

In your project folder, run: npx vercel

2
Follow the 3 Prompts

Press Enter to accept default settings. Vercel automatically detects Vite + React.

3
Instant Live URL

You will get an address like https://ayucase.vercel.app. You can bookmark this on your phone, tablet, and PC!

Option 2: Deploy to Netlify Drag & Drop or CLI

Run npm.cmd run build to create the dist folder, then run npx netlify deploy --prod and select the dist directory. Your site goes live immediately with free SSL!
